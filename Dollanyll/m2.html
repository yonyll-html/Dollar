What can you buy for a dollar?

This webpage is the list of the most obvious to the least obvious thing you could buy with a budget of a dollar.

List of things you can buy for a dollar

  • A pack of gum
  • A candy bar
  • A small bottle of water
  • A single piece of fruit
  • A postcard
  • A pen or pencil
  • A small notebook
  • A sticker or temporary tattoo
  • A small toy or trinket
  • A single serving of instant noodles
